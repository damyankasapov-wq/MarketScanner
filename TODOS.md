- [ ] **Splits and dividends in multi-day holds (medium)** — found by the /ship adversarial review on 2026-11-17. The archive stores raw prices (Alpaca `adjustment=raw`, yfinance `auto_adjust=False`), and the engine follows trades across sessions with gap fills. A hold across a split gaps to a fake stop (e.g. `--markets AAPL --from 2020-08-01` over the 4:1 split), and ex-dividend gaps on SPY/QQQ hurt overnight longs without crediting the dividend. The Alpaca backfill makes 10-year runs possible, so this matters more now.
